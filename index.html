What you get in the beta

Everything from inquiry to encore.

Every feature Oncore offers today — beta testers run their real bookings with all of it from day one.

Project overview

Client and event details, payments, messages and activity for every booking — all on one screen.

Lead capture

Put an inquiry form on your website. New leads land in your pipeline instantly — no copying from email.

Pipelines

Start from the Mobile DJ template or name your own stages. Drag events across a board or work from a list.

Automated workflows

Answer new inquiries the moment they arrive and send follow-ups on schedule — in your own words, without lifting a finger.

Scheduling

No more Calendly. Create unlimited meeting types — consultations, final calls, walkthroughs — and let clients book themselves.

Calendar sync

Booked meetings come with add-to-calendar invites, so they land on your calendar and your client’s with the event details attached.

Contracts

Build your contract once in your own words. Clients sign online, and the booking moves forward on its own.

Online payments

Collect deposits and final balances by card through Stripe. Connect the account you already have, or set up a new one.

Planning links

Every event gets its own planning link. Clients open it — no password — and fill in a short questionnaire you control.

Timeline builder

Client answers become a draft timeline. Drag moments into place on your final call, then share it or download a PDF.

Music management

Clients search songs or import their Spotify playlists right into the event, so every must-play is in one place.

Reviews

Sync your Google and WeddingWire reviews and show them off with a widget on your website.

Client planning

From questionnaire to timeline to playlist.

No more Google Forms, hand-built spreadsheets and copy-pasted song lists. One planning link per event, and the answers become your working timeline.

  • A light, inclusive questionnaire. Organized by the moments of the day, with questions that work for every couple.
  • A drag-and-drop timeline. Bring it to the final consultation call, adjust live, then share it or download a PDF.
  • Music, all in one place. Songs and Spotify playlists your clients add go straight into the event.

Questions

What does a beta tester do?+

Use Oncore with your real bookings and tell us what works and what doesn’t. Your feedback goes straight to the founder and shapes what we build next.

Can I use the Stripe account I already have?+

Yes — connect your existing account, or set up a new one during onboarding.

I’m switching from another CRM. What happens to my clients?+

Import them and set each client’s stage as you go, so their workflows pick up right where they left off.

Can I change the pipeline stages?+

Completely. Use one pipeline from inquiry to completed, or separate sales and planning pipelines — named your way.

Is Oncore only for DJs?+

It’s built for mobile DJs first. Planners, photographers, videographers and other event pros are next.
